Welcome to LITTLE EDEN Society

LITTLE EDEN Society is a south african non-profit organisation dedicated to providing care,support and a safe environment for people with profound intelletual disabilities.

The organisation wroks with families, volunteeras, donors, sponsors and communities to improve the quality of life or its residents.
